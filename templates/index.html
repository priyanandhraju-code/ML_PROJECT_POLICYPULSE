Welcome to PolicyPulse

PolicyPulse is a data analysis project that explores customer and vehicle insurance information and studies customer response to an insurance offer.

Dataset Information

  • Customer ID
  • Gender
  • Age
  • Driving License
  • Region Code
  • Previously Insured
  • Vehicle Age
  • Vehicle Damage
  • Annual Premium
  • Policy Sales Channel
  • Vintage
  • Response

Project Objective

To explore customer and vehicle-related factors and understand their relationship with insurance policy response.
